# Data dictionary: provenance

The `provenance` module holds `Source`, the provenance record that every fact in
Yakhnama links to: a citizen, an organisation, a government office, a news outlet, a
satellite, a research paper or a dataset. It was introduced in Phase 3. Column
conventions are in [README.md](README.md). **Proposed** marks a default the maintainer
has not confirmed yet. The open questions at the end of this page list each one.

Source code: `src/yakhnama/modules/provenance/domain/`.

## Principles

- **Every fact carries a source.** Impact claims, events and reports point at a
  source through a `SourceRef` (the source's id). They never copy the source.
- **A source is immutable once referenced.** The first time a fact cites a source,
  `is_referenced` becomes `true`. After that, every change is refused with
  `SourceImmutableError`. A correction is registered as a new source, so the
  provenance of facts that already cite the old one never changes under them.
- **Citizen sources must not identify the person.** A `citizen` source must not carry
  a URL or citation that identifies the reporter, such as a social-media profile, a
  phone number or a full name. This rule is documented for moderators and importers
  only. The domain cannot tell a person's name from a place name, so it does not try
  to enforce it (**proposed**, Q-P3).
- **Free text never travels in events.** Titles, citations, URLs, publishers and
  licence texts stay on the source. Events carry ids, types and field names only.

## Source (aggregate root)

| field | type | unit | meaning | provenance | since |
|-------|------|------|---------|------------|-------|
| `id` | `UUID` (v7) | — | Stable identity of the source. | Generated by the platform's `IdGenerator` at registration. | Phase 3 |
| `source_type` | `SourceType` | — | The kind of source (see the table below). It is fixed at registration, because it feeds the best-figure source ranking (**proposed**, Q-P5). | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `title` | `str` (1–300, safe text) | — | Short human-readable title, for example "PMD daily bulletin, 2022-08-26". | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `citation` | `str` (1–1000, safe text) | — | How to cite the source, for example a bibliographic reference or "Field report, Passu, 2022". For `citizen` sources it must not identify the person (Q-P3). | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `url` | `str` (1–2048), nullable | — | Where the source can be found online. It must be `http` or `https`, have a host and a valid port if any, contain no user information (`user:password@`) and be printable ASCII. Other schemes such as `javascript:`, `data:` and `file:` are refused (**proposed**, Q-P2). It is stored verbatim. | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `licence` | `Licence`, nullable | — | The terms under which the source may be reused, if known (see below). | The registering moderator or importer, from the source's own terms. | Phase 3 |
| `retrieved_at` | `DateWithPrecision`, nullable | — | When the source was retrieved or accessed, with how precisely that is known (`exact` … `year`). | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `publisher` | `str` (1–200, safe text), nullable | — | Who published the source, for example an agency, newspaper or data provider. | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `language` | `LanguageCode`, nullable | — | Language of the source's content (BCP 47 subset, for example `en`, `ur`, `ur-Arab`). | The registering moderator or importer. | Phase 3 |
| `owner_actor_id` | `UUID` (v7), nullable | — | The user who registered the source. It is `null` when the system registered it, for example an importer. | The authenticated actor, or the system. | Phase 3 |
| `organization_id` | `UUID` (v7), nullable | — | The organisation the source was registered for, if any. Whether the actor may act for it is checked by the identity policies. | The registering actor's request. | Phase 3 |
| `is_referenced` | `bool` | — | `true` once any fact cites the source. From then on the source can no longer change. It never goes back to `false`. | Set by `Source.mark_referenced` when a fact first cites the source. | Phase 3 |
| `version` | `int` (1 to 2 147 483 647) | count | Optimistic-concurrency version. It is 1 at registration and goes up by 1 with every change that has an effect, including `mark_referenced`. | Computed by the aggregate. | Phase 3 |
| `created_at` | `datetime` (UTC) | — | When the source was registered. This is a system timestamp, so it has no `DatePrecision`. | Platform `Clock`. | Phase 3 |
| `updated_at` | `datetime` (UTC) | — | When the source last changed. It is never earlier than `created_at`. | Platform `Clock`. | Phase 3 |

### Changes

| method | effect | refused when |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| `SourceFactory.register(source_type, details, owner)` | Creates an unreferenced source at version 1. Duplicate detection (same URL or citation) belongs to the command handler; two retrievals of one page may be two sources. | The details break a field rule. |
| `Source.update_details(details)` | Replaces every descriptive field (`title`, `citation`, `url`, `licence`, `retrieved_at`, `publisher`, `language`). A field left `null` is cleared. The same details give no change and no event. | The source is referenced (`SourceImmutableError`), even when the details are unchanged. |
| `Source.mark_referenced()` | Sets `is_referenced`. Calling it again is a no-op with no event, so every citing fact may call it. | Never. |

## SourceType

| value | meaning | status |
|-------|---------|--------|
| `citizen` | A person reporting what they saw. Must not be identifiable through the source (Q-P3). | glossary |
| `organisation` | A non-governmental organisation, for example an NGO or a community organisation. | glossary |
| `government` | A government office or agency, for example PMD or a district disaster management authority. | glossary |
| `news` | A news outlet or article. | glossary |
| `satellite` | A satellite image or product. | glossary |
| `research` | A research paper, report or thesis. | glossary |
| `dataset` | A published dataset, for example an inventory or a humanitarian dataset. | glossary |

## Licence (value object)

A licence has exactly one of the two fields (**proposed** model, Q-P1).

| field | type | unit | meaning | provenance | since |
|-------|------|------|---------|------------|-------|
| `spdx_id` | `str` (2–64 of `A-Z a-z 0-9 . + -`), nullable | — | An SPDX licence identifier, for example `CC-BY-4.0` or `ODbL-1.0`. Only the shape is checked, not membership of the SPDX list (**proposed**, Q-P1). | The source's own terms. | Phase 3 |
| `custom_text` | `str` (1–500, safe text), nullable | — | The terms of a licence that has no SPDX identifier, for example "Shared with Yakhnama for research use only" (**proposed**, Q-P4). | The source's own terms, as written by the moderator. | Phase 3 |

## Other value objects

| name | fields | meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| `SourceRef` | `source_id` (`UUID` v7) | What other modules store to cite a source. |
| `SourceOwner` | `actor_id`, `organization_id` (both nullable `UUID` v7) | Who registered a source and for which organisation. `SYSTEM_OWNER` has neither. |
| `SourceDetails` | `title`, `citation`, `url`, `licence`, `retrieved_at`, `publisher`, `language` | The descriptive fields, registered and updated together. |
| `RetrievalTime` | alias of `DateWithPrecision` | When a source was retrieved. |

## Text rules

`title`, `citation`, `publisher` and `custom_text` follow the shared safe-text rules
(`yakhnama.shared_kernel.text`): Unicode NFC, surrounding whitespace stripped, a
single line, and no control characters, lone surrogates, or bidirectional
embeddings, overrides and isolates (U+202A–U+202E, U+2066–U+2069). Lengths count
code points after normalisation.

## Events

Every event carries `event_id`, `occurred_at` (UTC), `aggregate_id`, `aggregate_type`
(`source`) and `version` (the source's version after the change). Payloads never
carry a title, citation, URL, publisher or licence text.

| event_type | extra fields | emitted when |
|------------|--------------|--------------|
| `provenance.source_registered` | `source_type`, `owner_actor_id`, `organization_id` | `SourceFactory.register`. |
| `provenance.source_details_updated` | `changed_fields` (names of the changed detail fields) | `Source.update_details` with a change. |
| `provenance.source_referenced` | — | `Source.mark_referenced` the first time. |

## Errors

| error | family | raised when |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| `SourceNotFoundError` | `not_found` | No source has the requested id. |
| `SourceImmutableError` | `invalid_transition` | A referenced source is asked to change. |
| `InvalidSourceUrlError` | `validation_error` | `parse_source_url` gets a URL that breaks the rules. The URL is never repeated in the error, because it may carry a token. |

## Persistence

`sources` (migration `0008_provenance`) stores one row per `Source`, with `licence`
as a JSONB object and `retrieved_at` split into an instant plus
`retrieved_at_precision` (both `NULL` together, enforced by a check constraint).
`owner_actor_id` and `organization_id` carry no foreign key, because users and
organisations belong to the `identity` module. Indexes: `source_type`,
`owner_actor_id`, and `(created_at, id)` for the newest-first keyset listing.

## Open questions raised by this module

| # | Question | Proposed default | Blocking |
|---|----------|------------------|----------|
| Q-P1 | How are licences modelled? Should SPDX ids be checked against the SPDX list, and are SPDX expressions (`MIT OR Apache-2.0`) needed? | One of an SPDX-shaped id or a custom text. Shape check only, no list, no expressions. | no |
| Q-P2 | Are the URL rules right: `http` allowed as well as `https`, printable ASCII only (IDNs punycoded), 2048 characters? | Yes to all three. | no |
| Q-P3 | How is a citizen source kept anonymous? Should the domain refuse URLs to social-media profiles, or citations that look like names or phone numbers? | A documented moderator rule only. The PII scrub of the reports triage chain may later suggest redactions for citations too. | no (privacy-relevant) |
| Q-P4 | Is a custom licence of up to 500 characters enough, or should long terms be stored as a document with a URL? | 500 characters, with the URL of the full terms in `url` where one exists. | no |
| Q-P5 | May `source_type` be corrected while a source is unreferenced? | No. It is fixed at registration; a wrong type means registering a new source. | no |
| Q-P6 | Should a source record who referenced it first, and when (`referenced_at`), or is the `provenance.source_referenced` event and the audit log enough? | The event and the audit log are enough. | no |
